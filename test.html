Cookie & Storage Policy
Last updated: 2025 · Powerhouse

We Don't Use Cookies

Powerhouse does not use HTTP cookies. We have no tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies of any kind.

What We Use Instead: localStorage

Powerhouse uses your browser's localStorage to store data locally on your device. This is similar to cookies but is never sent to a server automatically and is scoped entirely to this site.

What's Stored in localStorage

The following keys may be saved in your browser's localStorage:

ph_token — Your session token (used to keep you signed in for up to 30 days)

ph_username — Your username (for display before the token is verified)

ph_theme, ph_accent, ph_font — Your appearance preferences

ph_favorites, ph_history — Your local game favorites and play history

ph_eula, ph_welcomed — Whether you've accepted the EULA and seen the welcome screen

ph_notifs_seen — Which notifications you've already read

ph_pfp, ph_unclesai — Your selected profile picture and sidebar preferences

How to Clear Your Data

You can clear all localStorage data for Powerhouse by opening your browser's developer tools, navigating to Application → Local Storage, and deleting the entries. Alternatively, deleting your account from the Settings panel removes all server-side data.

Third-Party Storage

Powerhouse loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may set their own cookies in accordance with their privacy policy. No other third-party scripts or trackers are loaded.
